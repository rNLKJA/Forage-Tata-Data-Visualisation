# Choosing the right visuals

The second module gave five requests from the CEO and CMO and asked which chart would answer each one best. These are my choices, with the reason for each in brief.

| Request | My choice | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Revenue across the whole year, to see seasonal trends and plan for next year | Line chart | Time runs along the horizontal axis, so seasonal and cyclic patterns are easy to read. |
| The top 10 countries by revenue, with the split by product | Stacked bar chart | Each bar's length is a country's total revenue, and its segments show the products that make it up. |
| Average revenue per country, shown as a five-number summary | Box plot | It shows the minimum, quartiles, median and maximum, and makes spread, skew and outliers easy to spot. |
| The top 10 customers by revenue, from highest to lowest | Column chart | Sorted columns compare the customers directly, starting from the largest. |
| Demand for products in every country on a single view | Filled map | Shading each country by demand shows where it is highest without scrolling or hovering. |
